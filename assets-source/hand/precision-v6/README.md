# Thumb re-bound along the tracked neutral thumb

This candidate answers the owner's November 6 headset report: "where the thumb meets the palm crimps" while the thumb slides down the closed fingers on the palm side or across the open palm.

`pre-edit.blend` is a byte copy of `precision-v5/candidate.blend`. The owner's open `XR_hand.blend` is not touched.

- **Cause.** The authored thumb pointed sideways (+X) while the owner's relaxed tracked thumb points forward beside the index. The runtime re-aims each bone at its neutral joint, so `thumb_01` already sat 68° (left) to 75° (right) from its bind pose at the neutral pose, and reached 137–166° on crimp frames. Linear-blend skinning folds the wrist/`thumb_01` band past about 100°.
- **Bind direction.** One mesh serves both hands. The runtime basis is right-handed, so the left hand sees the authored depth axis flipped: the right neutral proximal thumb is (0.25, 0.92, +0.32) in glTF space and the left one (0.31, 0.90, −0.30). `rebind.py` uses the mirror average with the depth component zeroed, (0.29, 0.96, 0) for `thumb_01`/`thumb_02` (both follow `thumb-phalanx-proximal`) and (0.46, 0.89, 0) for `thumb_03`. Each hand keeps an 18° residual (21–23° for `thumb_03`) toward its own palm, measured by loading the exported GLB: 17.5/18.5° left/right for `thumb_01`, against 68.0/74.7° on precision-v5. The runtime needs no change.
- **Re-bind.** `rebind.py` poses `thumb_01`–`03` by the minimal swing onto those directions (70.3°, 4.5° and 6.8°), bakes the posed skin with dual-quaternion skinning so the new bind carries no linear-blend collapse at the root, and applies the pose as the rest. Bone names, parents, the 13 authored bones and every `restLength` are asserted unchanged before the helpers are added. Vertices with no thumb weight move less than 1e-7 m (measured 1.6e-8 m), and the lowest vertex, which sets the runtime wrist offset, is unchanged. An 8-pass Taubin relax of the baked root moved vertices by at most 0.02 mm, so it was dropped.
- **Actions.** Each thumb key is left-multiplied by the constant `newRelRest⁻¹ · oldRelRest`, which is exact on keys and handles because it is linear in the quaternion components. Every bone in all four actions keeps its armature-space pose at frames 1–37 (maximum error 0.0°). Open now keys the thumb back to its authored sideways pose, so `validate_export.py` no longer expects identity thumb keys in Open or Thumbs Up. It instead requires the reimported joints and thumb tip to match the candidate rig at frame 25 (largest gap 6.5e-8 m).
- **Root blend.** On a closed hand the opposing thumb root travels up to 30 mm (`hand-pose-constraints.ts`). `rebind.py` diffuses the wrist/`thumb_01` split over the 413 vertices within 60 mm of the `thumb_01` head (300 iterations, each vertex keeps its pair total, mean share change 0.14, max 0.47).
- **Heel taper.** `THUMB_TAPER` (default 25 mm) moves the `thumb_01` share behind the head off `thumb_01` with a smoothstep over 25 mm. It runs before the bake about the posed `thumb_01` head and axis (193 vertices), and after the blend about the new rest head and axis (235 vertices, largest share moved 0.48). The second pass hands the removed share to the heel helper rather than the wrist.
- **Pocket lift.** The bake deepened small pits on the back of the hand at the thumb-side palm edge. After the bake, `rebind.py` finds back-side, thumb-weighted vertices whose 2-ring pit deepened by more than 0.3 mm (27 seeds, worst 0.83 mm), grows them by two rings (97 vertices) and lifts each one outward along its normal by half its excess over the pre-bake pit, for 10 passes. Turning it off on an earlier helper configuration raised the thumbrest pit to 1.71 mm, so it stays.
- **Helper bones.** Three skin-only bones, `thumb_root_helper`, `thumb_web_helper` and `thumb_heel_helper`, are wrist children with exactly the `thumb_01` rest. They are not parents of anything, so `thumb_01` world transforms, Keyborb points and pinch lines are unchanged. Each frame, after the retarget and the opposition solver, the runtime sets each helper's local rotation to `slerp(rest, thumb_01, rotation)` and its position to `lerp(rest, thumb_01, travel)` (`THUMB_HELPERS` in `src/xr/rigged-hand.ts`). The controller path runs the same update.
  - **Root** (rotation 0.5, travel 1) and **web** (rotation 0.5, travel 0.55) helpers carry the wrist/`thumb_01` band. A vertex with thumb share `t` becomes wrist `1−2t` + helper `2t` below 0.5 and helper `2−2t` + `thumb_01` `2t−1` above, so the nominal turn is unchanged but no vertex blends bones more than half a turn apart. The re-expression fades out over 15 mm ahead of the head, so the thumb shaft stays on `thumb_01` (432 vertices). The helper share splits between root and web with a 15 mm smoothstep across the head, where each millimetre toward the index counts 0.5 mm toward the web side, so the back of the web between thumb and index follows the smaller web travel.
  - **Heel** (rotation 0, travel 1) carries the tapered heel share. It moves with the opposing root but does not swing out past the palm edge.
  - **Why three.** One half-turn helper with half travel cut the left closed-slide flips to 3 but grew the closed-fist lobe (outline excess 1,185 mm² at frame 180 against v6b's 889). Full travel on the band removed the lobe (209 mm²) but sheared the web into 16 flips. A turning heel (rotation 0.25 to 0.5) swung the heel out again. Splitting web from root and giving the heel travel only met every crease bound with the lobe at 257 mm².
  - **Actions.** The root and web helpers are keyed in all four actions at `thumb_01`'s key frames with half its key rotation, so a clip consumer sees the runtime blend. The heel helper has no keys because actions carry no root travel. The runtime does not play the clips.
- **Fold corrective (v11, per side since v12).** Two shape keys, `thumb_fold_right` and `thumb_fold_left`, round the closed-fist thumb root back toward precision-v5's outline. The runtime retarget is side-asymmetric (aligned rest, thumb neutral and pole side differ per hand), so the left fist bulges on different vertices than the right, and v11's single shared key left the left knob and the pit under it in place. Each hand drives only its own key (`THUMB_FOLD_MORPHS`) and holds the other at 0; each hand is its own `createRiggedHand` instance with its own parse of the asset, so `morphTargetInfluences` is per hand. The keys move skin only: bones, Keyborb points and the pinch, close and fist lines are unchanged.
  - **Weight.** `hand-pose-constraints.ts` exposes `foldTravel`, the opposition root travel times the finger closure, reset to 0 at the start of every update. The runtime takes `smoothstep(foldTravel, THUMB_FOLD_START 4 mm, THUMB_FOLD_FULL 15 mm)` as the target in the helper update path (`src/xr/rigged-hand.ts`) and, since v12, eases the influence toward it with `1 − exp(−Δt / THUMB_FOLD_SMOOTHING)`, a 100 ms time constant in v12 and 85 ms since v13. The first tracked frame after a hide, lost tracking, a controller frame or a side change takes the target directly. The weights below are settled targets (v11's unsmoothed weight). A pinch with open fingers and controller mode have no closure-weighted travel, so they get 0. Measured weight: slide frame 110 → 0, 156 → 0 (the thumb has not folded yet, travel 0), 159 → 0.13, 162 → 1, 180 → 1, 204 → 0.68, 230 → 1; thumb-rest frame 75 → 1 on both hands; left slide 180/230 → 1, 204 → 0.53. A pinch with partly closed fingers can reach about 0.3 (motion capture frame 180). With a 20 mm full point, thumb-rest frame 75 only reached 0.87/0.79.

  - **Skin-tied rate (v13).** Easing alone still let the key move faster than the skin when the hand was nearly still: on thumb-rest frame 35 the key moved 1.4 mm while the skin under it moved 0.7. Each frame the runtime now clamps the weight change to `max(THUMB_FOLD_SKIN_RATIO · skinStep / reach, THUMB_FOLD_MIN_RATE · Δt)`, with a ratio of 0.6 and a floor of 0.12 per second. `skinStep` is the largest unmorphed skin motion since the last frame at the key's 16 most-displaced vertices, and `reach` is the key's largest delta. The clamp is applied after the easing. Resets still take the target directly. Measured at the captured stamps on thumb-rest frames 30–40, the key's per-capture motion over the skin's is at most 0.37 in the replay test (v12: 2.16), and the reviewer's lag measure gives at most 0.4 on the right and 0.57 on the left. The right key's fill (below) raised its largest delta from 7.1 to 8.4 mm, so the time constant dropped from 100 to 85 ms to keep the slide's lag: at frame 161 the surface is 2.8 mm short on the right and 2.3 mm on the left, and at most 0.3 mm by frame 164.

  - **Shape.** `fold_morph.ts` fits one key from one hand's captures: `thumb_fold_right` from the right slide (156–235) and thumb-rest (0–149) frames whose settled weight is at least 0.3 (176 frames), `thumb_fold_left` from the true-mirrored left ones (172 frames). Right: 15 smoothing and 6 relaxation passes, fade over z 2–10 mm. Left: 8 and 6 passes, fade over z 10–18 mm, so the fit reaches the left knob, which sits palm-side at bind z +3 to +10 mm (vertices 643, 644, 2675, 2676, 2828, 2833–2835, carried 0.37–0.71 by the root helper). It pulls each posed correction back to bind space through the vertex's blended skin matrix L (mesh-local, so the left hand's mirrored root cancels) and solves the weighted least-squares fit `d = (Σ w² LᵀL)⁻¹ Σ w Lᵀ Δ`. Since v13 the right key also has a fill term (`fill: 3` in the fit table). In each frame it takes the back view's thumb-side edge from 40 mm below to 30 mm above the `thumb_01` head and pushes the rim skin toward the edge's concave envelope. The push is at most 6 mm per frame, fades over 15 mm behind the rim and is spread over one neighbour pass. It is fitted as its own least-squares term over only the frames that push each vertex, because frames where a vertex is off the rim would otherwise average the fill away. The left key keeps `fill: 0`. Fills of 1, 2 and 3 on the left raised the flipped band triangles on the real left fists of the motion capture (frames 214–215 and 251–255) from 21 to 27, 30 and 36. On the right, fills of 1.3–2 without the neighbour pass deepened the pit to 2.1–3.0 mm and flipped up to 10 band triangles, and a fill of 4 with two neighbour passes flipped 4. The right key moves 461 vertices by at most 8.4 mm (304 and 7.1 mm in v12), the left key 397 by at most 6.6 mm. glTF stores each target as sparse accessors (right: 461 POSITION and 1,036 NORMAL entries; left: 397 and 950), so the GLB is 256,068 bytes (v12: 251,756). three.js expands them and morphs every vertex. The contact probes read the morphed skin: at full weight the left key moves 33 probe vertices by up to 6.6 mm and the right key 39 by up to 8.4 mm (v12: 25, up to 5.9 mm; v11's shared key: 25, up to 5.3 mm).

  - **Export.** `corrective.py` adds the key to `candidate.blend` at value 0 (each JSON bind position matches exactly one Blender vertex within 1e-5 m). `corrective.py` adds `thumb_fold_right` then `thumb_fold_left`; the glTF primitive carries two POSITION/NORMAL targets, the mesh `weights` is `[0, 0]` and `extras.targetNames` names both keys. three.js keeps them in `morphTargetDictionary`. ## Regeneration

`rebind.py` reads `src/xr/hand-neutral-reference.json`, writes `rebound.blend` (re-bound, v5 weights), `candidate.blend` and `rebind.json`. `BLEND_RADIUS`, `BLEND_ITERATIONS`, `BAKE_DQS`, `BIND_TURN`, `BLEND_BELOW`, `THUMB_TAPER`, `POCKET_PASSES`, `HELPER`, `HELPER_AHEAD`, `HELPER_SPLIT`, `HELPER_INWARD` and `HELPER_TAPER` override the defaults. `REBIND_OUT` redirects the outputs. `corrective.py` adds `thumb_fold_right` and `thumb_fold_left` from the matching JSON files (`CORRECTIVE_OUT` overrides the output) and saves `candidate.blend`. Re-running the chain reproduces the staged GLB byte for byte. `export_action_objs.py -- <dir>` writes each action's hold-frame mesh as OBJ for `.local/tools/render_posed.py`.
